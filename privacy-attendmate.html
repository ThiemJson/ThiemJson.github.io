Privacy Policy for AttendMate: Attendance Tracker

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AttendMate: Attendance Tracker ("AttendMate", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Application. AttendMate is designed to provide an offline-first attendance and timetable experience. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the Application. Core attendance data is stored locally on your device. Limited technical and usage information may be processed through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to help us understand Application usage and improve stability.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is created by you or generated through your use of AttendMate and is stored locally on your device:

  • semesters, subjects, acronyms, colors, attendance targets, and schedules;
  • attendance records and manual attendance baselines;
  • planned leave and holiday records;
  • rooms, buildings, classroom names, and saved classroom coordinates;
  • notification schedules and attendance reminder settings;
  • timetable import and export data;
  • semester backup files;
  • local diagnostic logs; and
  • application preferences and onboarding status.

AttendMate does not require this information to be uploaded to an AttendMate account or server. We do not operate a cloud account system for attendance records. This locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it within the Application, delete exported files or backups, clear the Application’s storage, or uninstall the Application. Files that you export or share are handled by the destination you choose and may remain outside the Application.

2. Analytics and Crash Diagnostics

We use analytics and crash-reporting services to understand usage and improve reliability. Our current relevant service provider is Google (Firebase).

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics helps us understand general Application usage, such as feature interactions, navigation events, Application version, device and operating system information, and review-related actions. Depending on the platform and Firebase configuration, Firebase may process device or installation identifiers, approximate technical location derived from an IP address, and other usage information. We do not intentionally send subject names, attendance records, saved classroom coordinates, timetable contents, leave entries, or backup contents as Analytics events.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics helps us identify crashes and technical failures. Crash reports may include crash stack traces, device model, operating system version, Application version, installation identifiers, memory or execution state, and other diagnostic information associated with a failure. We do not intentionally attach attendance records, timetable contents, classroom coordinates, or backup contents to Crashlytics reports.

For more information about Google processing, see Google’s privacy resources: How Google uses information, Google Privacy Policy, and Firebase Privacy and Security.

3. Location Information

AttendMate includes optional location and geofencing features. If you choose to use them, the Application may request:

  • Location while using the Application, to capture your current position when saving classroom coordinates;
  • Background location, to perform optional location-assisted attendance checks near scheduled class times.

Saved classroom coordinates and the results of attendance checks are stored locally on your device. Location access is optional — you can enter coordinates manually, deny location permission, or disable location services and continue using the core timetable and attendance features. Background execution is controlled by the operating system and may be affected by battery or permission settings.

4. Notifications

AttendMate may request permission to send local attendance reminders. Notifications are generated and scheduled on your device. AttendMate does not currently use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send remote marketing notifications. You can disable notification permission through your device settings.

5. Backup, Import, Export, and Sharing

AttendMate can create and restore local semester backups and can import or export timetable information in supported formats such as JSON or CSV, and may create PDF summaries. Backup and export files remain under your control. When you choose to save, share, or open a file using another application or storage provider, that third party may process the file under its own privacy policy. Review destinations carefully before sharing.

AttendMate retains up to three rolling backup copies in local Application storage where supported.

6. In-App Reviews and External Links

AttendMate may use the platform in-app review interface. The operating system and the relevant store control review prompts and process any rating or review you submit. The Application may open external pages; your interaction with those services is governed by their privacy policies.

7. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • provide attendance, timetable, reminder, backup, and optional location features;
  • maintain and improve Application performance and reliability;
  • understand aggregate feature usage;
  • detect, investigate, and fix crashes or technical problems;
  • protect the Application against misuse or security threats; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use attendance records or classroom coordinates for advertising.

8. Service Providers and Disclosure

We may allow service providers to process limited information only as needed to provide their services. Our current relevant service provider is Google LLC through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with a legal obligation, protect rights, investigate fraud or security incidents, or support an acquisition subject to appropriate notice and protections.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Local data

You can remove individual records using delete controls in AttendMate. You can remove all local Application data by clearing Application storage where supported or uninstalling the Application. Exported files must be deleted separately from every location where you saved or shared them.

Firebase data

Analytics and Crashlytics information is retained according to our Firebase configuration and Google’s retention practices. Because AttendMate does not provide user accounts and Firebase data may be associated with technical installation identifiers, we may not be able to identify or retrieve a particular record based only on your name or email address.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by AttendMate. Local information is also protected by the security controls of your device and operating system. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure; you are responsible for protecting access to your device and choosing secure destinations when exporting files.

11. Children’s Privacy

AttendMate is intended as a general attendance-management utility for students and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request names, contact details, or account registration information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us in a way that is not described in this Policy, contact us.

12. International Data Processing

Google and its service infrastructure may process Analytics and Crashlytics information in countries other than the country where you live. Core attendance, timetable, saved classroom, leave, and local backup data remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

13. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. You can control many Application permissions directly via system settings and submit privacy requests to us at the contact below.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when AttendMate’s features, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change. We will update the Last updated date and publish the revised Policy at the same location. Where required we may provide additional notice in the Application.
